Church Partnerships

Stronger Churches. Healthier Families. Greater Community Impact.

Pathways Charitable Foundation partners with churches and faith-based organizations to create sustainable pathways to healthcare, wellness resources, and support for families—while helping churches raise funds to further their own mission.

Become a Church Partner

More Than a Fundraiser

A Partnership With Purpose.

Churches have always been a place where families turn during life’s most important moments.

Pathways Charitable Foundation believes churches should have access to resources that help them respond when their members and surrounding communities need support.

Our Church Partnership creates an opportunity for your church to raise funds while connecting families with valuable healthcare and wellness resources.

And the impact goes even further.

Funds generated through Pathways’ participation help us continue giving back through donated healthcare memberships, health and wellness scans, resource navigation, education, and assistance for families in need.

How The Partnership Works

1

Your Church Partners With Pathways

We work with your church leadership to establish a community health and fundraising campaign designed for your congregation.

2

Families Get Connected

Church members, families, friends, and community supporters are introduced to participating healthcare membership options and available wellness resources.

3

Your Church Raises Funds

Qualifying participation through your church’s campaign generates fundraising support for your church.

Funds can help support ministry programs, outreach efforts, community initiatives, and other church priorities.

4

Pathways Gives Back

The impact continues beyond the fundraiser. Pathways uses resources generated through our programs and partnerships to help provide:

  • Donated healthcare memberships for families in need
  • Health and wellness scans
  • Resource navigation
  • Education and community outreach
  • Connections to additional resources and assistance
5

Together, We Create Another Pathway

Every participating church becomes part of a larger mission to strengthen families and expand access to resources in communities that need them.

Community Health & Wellness Days

Bring Pathways Into Your Church.

Participating churches may have opportunities to host a Pathways Community Health & Wellness Day for their congregation and surrounding community.

Depending on the event, activities may include:

  • Health and wellness scans
  • Healthcare resource education
  • Information about available healthcare membership options
  • Community resource navigation
  • Opportunities to identify families who may need additional assistance
  • Connections to participating community resources

These events can help churches turn their facilities into a point of connection where families can learn, ask questions, discover resources, and find their next pathway.

The Healthcare Connection

Helping Families Find Another Way to Access Care.

KonnectMD

As part of the Church Partnership, Pathways may connect participating families with healthcare membership resources available through KonnectMD.

KonnectMD offers healthcare membership options designed to help individuals and families access participating healthcare services and resources.

Families decide for themselves whether a membership is appropriate for their needs.

There is never an obligation to enroll in order to participate in Pathways’ community outreach or request resource assistance.
