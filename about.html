Why Pathways Exists

Pathways Charitable Foundation was created to help individuals and families who feel overwhelmed by healthcare costs, limited access, or uncertainty about where to turn.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create pathways to affordable healthcare, education, support, and hope for individuals and families in need.

Our Vision

We envision communities where no one has to face healthcare challenges without information, support, or a path forward.

Our Philosophy

  • We begin with a person.
  • We listen before we speak.
  • We educate before we recommend.
  • We serve without expectation.

Meet Our Founder

Angela Thomas

Angela Thomas founded Pathways Charitable Foundation with a simple belief: people deserve to be heard, informed, and treated with dignity when searching for healthcare solutions.

Her passion is helping others navigate the complexities of healthcare and connecting them with resources that can make a real difference in their lives.
